ABOUT US

Less scrolling. Better finds.

The Find List is an independent shopping and lifestyle publication built around a simple idea: the things we recommend should be useful, appealing, and easy to understand.

What we do

We organize home, lifestyle, seasonal, dorm, beauty, wellness, and technology finds into clear guides. Instead of presenting an endless catalog, we explain why an item may be useful, who it is best for, and what to check before ordering.

How we choose finds

We look for products with a clear purpose, strong visual appeal, practical everyday value, and the ability to fit naturally into a helpful story or collection. Trends can introduce us to a product, but usefulness determines whether it belongs on the list.

How the site is supported

The Find List participates in affiliate programs. When a reader follows a qualifying affiliate link and makes a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to the reader. Compensation does not change the price paid by the reader.

Our promise: We aim to make affiliate relationships clear and keep editorial explanations useful. Readers should always make purchasing decisions based on their own needs, budget, research, and the latest product information.

Our content

Product details, availability, and seller information can change. We link readers to the current retailer listing to verify those details. The Find List does not manufacture, sell, ship, or provide warranties for the products featured.
