Fall Mantle Decor Ideas

A warm, layered mantle can make the whole room feel like fall. The easiest look combines greenery, candlelight, a few sculptural pieces and just enough seasonal color.

Easy formulaShop the lookStyling tipsMore fall decorations
The easy fall mantle formula:

Start with one tall anchor, add a loose leaf garland, group candles at two or three heights, then finish with a few pumpkins or ceramic accents. Keep some empty space so the mantle feels styled instead of crowded.

Make it look intentional

3 quick styling rules

Vary the heights. Combine one tall element with medium and low objects so the eye moves across the display. Repeat colors, not objects. Two or three repeated tones look more polished than matching pairs everywhere. Leave breathing room. A little empty space is what keeps a layered mantle from looking cluttered.

If you use a working fireplace, keep candles, garlands, fabrics and other flammable decor safely away from heat and open flame.
