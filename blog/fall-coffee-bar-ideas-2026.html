Create Your Dream Fall Coffee Bar

1. Start with the coffee maker

Anchor the station with the machine you use every day. Keep it centered and leave enough room to prep your drink.

2. Add café-style foam and seasonal syrups

A milk frother plus pumpkin spice, gingerbread and toasted marshmallow gives you an instant seasonal drink menu.

3. Make the mugs part of the décor

Mix practical mugs with one or two statement pieces. Pumpkin mugs bring seasonal color while our Find List mugs add cozy sayings and designs.

4. Organize the ingredients

Keep beans or grounds in an airtight canister and add cinnamon, sugar or toppings nearby without overcrowding the counter.

5. Don't forget iced coffee

Ribbed glass cups make the same station work for iced pumpkin coffee and cold brew while the weather is still warm.

The Fall Coffee Bar Formula:
Coffee maker + frother + seasonal syrup + favorite mug + cozy fall detail ☕🍂
