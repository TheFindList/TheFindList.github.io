HALLOWEEN 2026 • DECOR GUIDE

How to Make Your House Look Haunted

Eight easy layers for turning a normal home into a moody, spooky Halloween house—without making it feel like a party-store aisle.

The best haunted spaces are less about filling every corner with decorations and more about atmosphere. Start with lighting, create a few spooky focal points, then layer ghosts, pumpkins and eerie details where they will have the most impact.

01 • LIGHTING

Turn off the big lights

Overhead lighting instantly kills the haunted-house mood. Use warm lamps, dimmable accent lights and flameless candles instead. Let a few corners stay intentionally dark.

02 • ENTRYWAY

Make the front door the first scare

Cluster pumpkins and a lantern near the door, add wispy faux cobwebs around the frame, and use one ghost or spooky silhouette as the focal point.

03 • GHOSTS

Hide little ghosts around the house

Place glowing ceramic ghosts on bookshelves, side tables and the mantel. Repeating one character in unexpected places makes the whole house feel connected.

04 • MANTEL

Build a haunted mantel

Drape a fall garland across the shelf, weave in tiny lights, then mix pumpkins, flameless candles and darker Halloween accents. Keep the arrangement asymmetrical for a slightly eerie look.

05 • WALLS

Add bats, shadows and cobwebs

A swarm of removable black bats climbing one wall creates more impact than dozens of tiny decorations. Stretch faux cobwebs thinly across mirrors and frames rather than using thick clumps.

06 • CANDLELIGHT

Create flickering corners

Group flameless candles in odd numbers at different heights. Place them beside old books, pumpkins or a lantern to make shelves and consoles look mysteriously lit.

07 • COLOR

Limit the palette

Stick to black, cream, burnt orange and one deep accent such as burgundy or forest green. A controlled palette keeps the Halloween decorations looking intentional.

08 • FINAL LAYER

Add one surprise in every room

A ghost on a bathroom shelf, bats above the bed or a tiny pumpkin beside the coffee maker makes the theme feel like it has taken over the whole house.

THE EASY FORMULA

Dark corners + warm glow + repeated spooky details

You do not need dozens of decorations. Repeat three or four elements—ghosts, pumpkins, candles and black silhouettes—throughout the house and let lighting do most of the work.
