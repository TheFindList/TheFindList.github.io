How to Build the Perfect Boo Basket 👻

Cozy, cute, useful—and way more personal than a bag of candy.

Think of a boo basket as Halloween's answer to an Easter basket: a small collection of treats, cozy extras and seasonal surprises for your girlfriend, boyfriend, best friend, roommate, family member or neighbor.

The easiest boo-basket formula

🧺 1. Start with the container

A woven basket, pumpkin pail, reusable tote or even an oversized mug works. Smaller containers are easier to make look full.

🧸 2. Add one hero gift

Pick one item that makes the basket feel special: a cozy blanket, tumbler, candle or self-care favorite.

🍫 3. Fill in the fun stuff

Add favorite candy, popcorn, hot cocoa, fuzzy socks or another inexpensive seasonal treat.

🎃 4. Make it Halloween

Finish with a tiny pumpkin, ghost decoration, orange ribbon or handwritten “You've Been Booed” note.

The Find List tip: Don't make every item Halloween-only. One or two spooky touches plus things they'll actually keep using makes the basket feel much more thoughtful.

Three easy versions

Cozy basket: throw blanket + fall candle + hot cocoa + fuzzy socks + chocolate.

Girlfriend / bestie basket: tumbler + sugar scrub + heatless curling set + favorite candy + handwritten note.

Budget basket: one fall candle + popcorn + candy + socks + a small Halloween decoration. A small basket packed neatly can look better than an expensive oversized one.

When should you give it?

Any time leading up to Halloween works. Giving it earlier in October means the recipient gets more time to enjoy the seasonal pieces before October 31.
