Easy fall refresh

Where to use these pieces

Living room: start with the pillow covers and knit throw, then add one candle and a short piece of garland. Dining room: use the runner with a few candles and simple seasonal stems. Entryway: layer garland around a mirror or console and add one warm-toned candle. Bedroom: repeat the same burnt-orange accent on the bed or reading chair so the room feels connected to the rest of the house.

The trick is repetition, not quantity. Choose two or three colors and repeat them in different textures around the home instead of filling every surface with decorations.
