Fall Mantle

The easiest formula: tall anchor + low garland + grouped candles + two or three pumpkins.

  • Mix matte and natural textures
  • Repeat 2–3 colors
  • Leave breathing room around the focal point

Fall Mantle Decor

For a collected look, combine eucalyptus, fall leaves, ceramic accents and soft candlelight instead of filling every inch.

  • Use odd-numbered groupings
  • Vary object heights
  • Carry one accent color across the room

Fall Fireplace Decor

Bring the styling below the mantle with a basket, folded throw, lantern or stacked pumpkins to frame the fireplace.

  • Keep the hearth uncluttered
  • Balance both sides without matching exactly
  • Keep flammable decor safely away from heat and flame
