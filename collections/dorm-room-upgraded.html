Dorm Room, Upgraded

A realistic plan for making a small shared room work harder without making it feel crowded.

Dorm rooms ask one small footprint to be a bedroom, study space, closet, kitchen, and social spot. The answer is not simply buying more storage. It is choosing pieces that use vertical space, keep daily necessities within reach, and make resetting the room fast.

Start with the problems that happen every day: nowhere to put the small stuff, crowded outlets, laundry taking over the floor, and a closet that runs out of room. Solve those first, and the whole space becomes easier to live in.

01 · Go vertical

Use height before floor space

A rolling cart creates several useful surfaces in the footprint of one. It can hold snacks and dishes, become a beauty station, or organize school supplies beside a desk. Because it moves, it can adapt when the room layout changes.
